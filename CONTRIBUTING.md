# 🏛️ contributing

## xenia

xenia (ξενία) is the ancient Greek sacred law of hospitality — the bond between host and guest. a host provides generously for their guest; a guest honours the host's home without overstepping their welcome.

hephaestus is a personal tool, and this forge is mine. you are welcome here — take what's made, use it well, adapt it for yourself. but the bellows stay where they are.

i am not accepting pull requests or direct contributions to this repository.

## what you can do

- **fork** — clone it, adapt it, make your own version for your own use. the MIT license permits this freely.
- **report bugs** — open an issue if you find something genuinely broken. no guarantees on response time or resolution.
- **use the api** — the engine ships a [public programmatic api](src/index.ts) if you want to build on top of it.

## what you can't do

- submit pull requests (they will be closed without review)

thanks for your interest. go build something worth forging.
